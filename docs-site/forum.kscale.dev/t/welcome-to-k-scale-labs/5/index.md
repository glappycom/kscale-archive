> ## K-Scale Labs
>
> A place to discuss how to move humanity up the Kardashev scale

Here are some things you can do to get started:

![:speaking_head:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/speaking_head.png?v=12 ":speaking_head:") **Introduce yourself** by adding your picture and information about yourself and your interests to [your profile](/my/preferences/account). What is one thing you’d like to be asked about?

![:open_book:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/open_book.png?v=12 ":open_book:") **Get to know the community** by [browsing discussions](/latest) that are already happening here. When you find a post interesting, informative, or entertaining, use the ![:heart:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/heart.png?v=12 ":heart:") to show your appreciation or support!

![:handshake:](https://emoji.discourse-cdn.com/twitter/handshake.png?v=12 ":handshake:") **Contribute** by commenting, sharing your own perspective, asking questions, or offering feedback in the discussion. Before replying or starting new topics, please review the [Community Guidelines](/faq).

> If you need help or have a suggestion, feel free to ask in [Site Feedback](/c/site-feedback/2) or [contact the admins](/about).
