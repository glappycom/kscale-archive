# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9/29/2025

[Back to home](/)

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, your rights, and how you can control your information. We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable laws.

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Account Data:** Name, email, and password when you sign up.
* **Contact Data:** Email address and message content if you contact us.
* **Usage Data:** Pages visited, actions taken, device/browser info, IP address (anonymized where possible).
* **Cookies:** See our Cookie Policy below.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

* To provide and improve our services and products.
* To communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests.
* To analyze site usage and improve user experience.
* To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies to remember your preferences and analyze site traffic. Non-essential cookies (e.g., analytics) are only set with your consent. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time.

* **Essential Cookies:** Required for site functionality.
* **Analytics Cookies:** Help us understand how visitors use our site (set only if you accept cookies).

## 4. Data Sharing & Processors

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted third-party processors (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, email providers) who help us deliver our services. All such partners are GDPR-compliant and only process data as instructed by us.

## 5. International Data Transfers

If we transfer your data outside the EEA/UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place.

## 6. Data Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 7. Your Rights

* **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data.
* **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
* **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
* **Restriction:** Ask us to restrict processing of your data.
* **Objection:** Object to certain types of processing.
* **Portability:** Request your data in a portable format.
* **Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time.

To exercise your rights, contact us at [privacy@kscale.dev](mailto:privacy@kscale.dev).

## 8. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding your privacy, contact us at [privacy@kscale.dev](mailto:privacy@kscale.dev).
